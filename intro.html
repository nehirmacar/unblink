Chapter 03 · How it works

Three steps, all inside your browser.

The camera feed is analysed frame by frame on your own device. The moment the eyes switch from closed to open, a photo lands in your captures.

STEP 01

Find the face

Turn on the camera. UNBLINK locates your face and frames the eye region in the live view.

openness 0.00 · open
STEP 02

Watch the eyelids

It tracks your eyes in real time and labels them as open or closed, so you always see what the camera sees.

STEP 03

Open means shoot

Closed, then open: that change is the trigger. Auto capture saves the frame, and a manual button is there too.
